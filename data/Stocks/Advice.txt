- Tactics: Trade only the highest‑Sharpe, high‑belief symbols (MAR, GILD). Use a 1‑2 % risk‑per‑trade limit, tight stop‑losses (≈ 1 % below entry), and exit on loss of the “Bullish” sentiment signal (e.g., sudden sentiment shift or volume spike).  
- Position sizing: With a $1,000 budget, allocate up to $200 to a single intraday trade; rotate between MAR and GILD depending on liquidity.

 2. Swing (3‑10 days)  
- Objective: Ride the confirmed upward trend while allowing for modest pull‑backs.  
- Tactics: Enter on a break of the previous day’s high with a trailing stop set at 3‑4 % below the highest price achieved since entry. Favor stocks with a Sharpe > 0.8 and belief ≥ 70 (MAR, GILD, COST).  
- Position sizing: Split capital into 3‑4 positions (≈ $250 each). This balances exposure and diversification while keeping each trade within a 5 % portfolio risk envelope.
